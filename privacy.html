Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how E-DS ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our cross-border e-commerce assistant service (the "Service"), which integrates with third-party platforms including Pinterest, Amazon, Instagram, Facebook, and Google.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: When you sign in via a third-party platform (e.g. Pinterest OAuth), we receive your basic profile information (name, profile ID, avatar URL) and the permissions you explicitly grant.
  • Platform data: Public pin data, board data, search results, and trend signals retrieved from Pinterest and other connected platforms, used solely to provide you product keyword, competitor, and trend analysis.
  • Usage data: Service interaction logs (requests made, timestamps, error messages) for reliability and debugging.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (e.g. pin search, trend analysis, product listing optimization).
  • Authenticate your identity and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your Pinterest data for advertising, retargeting, or training third-party AI models.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize a specific integration or export.
  • Service providers: Trusted vendors that host our infrastructure (e.g. cloud hosting, error tracking) under strict data-protection agreements.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Our Service integrates with third-party platforms (Pinterest, Amazon, Instagram, Facebook, Google, Xiaohongshu). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, encrypted storage, access controls) to protect your data. No method of transmission over the Internet, however, is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.
